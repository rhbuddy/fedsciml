# Literature Survey: Federated Scientific Machine Learning

Survey of the 38 reference papers of the project "Federated scientific machine learning for approximating functions and solving differential equations with data heterogeneity" (Zhang, Liu, Lu — arXiv:2410.13141 / IEEE TNNLS 2025).

| # | Title | Journal / Year / Ref. No. | Methodology | Drawback | Description (Abstract) |
|---|-------|---------------------------|-------------|----------|--------------------------|
| 1 | Federated scientific machine learning for approximating functions and solving differential equations with data heterogeneity | IEEE Trans. Neural Netw. Learn. Syst., Vol. 36, No. 10, pp. 18104–18117, 2025 (DOI: 10.1109/TNNLS.2025.3580409) | FedPINN & FedDeepONet with FedAvg-Adam/FedOPT; 1-Wasserstein distance for heterogeneity quantification; weight-divergence theory | Performance degrades as heterogeneity (W1) increases; requires careful partitioned dataset construction | FedPINN & FedDeepONet for SciML under non-IID data; uses 1-Wasserstein metric and weight-divergence analysis; matches centralized accuracy. |
| 2 | DeepXDE: A Deep Learning Library for Solving Differential Equations | arXiv:1907.04502 / 2020 | Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) with automatic differentiation; residual-based adaptive refinement (RAR); constructive solid geometry | Slower than FEM for forward problems; no error bounds; hyperparameter-tuning dependent | DeepXDE library for PINNs with auto-diff and RAR; solves forward/inverse PDEs (fractional, stochastic) on complex CSG geometries. |
| 3 | Meta-learning PINN loss functions | arXiv:2107.05544 / 2021 | Gradient-based meta-learning (bi-level optimization) for offline loss function discovery; LAL/OAL parametrization | Differentiation over optimization path is expensive and prone to exploding gradients; design-choice dependent | Meta-learns PINN loss functions via bi-level optimization (LAL/OAL); offline losses generalize to OOD tasks and architectures. |
| 4 | Gradient-enhanced physics-informed neural networks for forward and inverse PDE problems | arXiv:2111.02801 / 2021 | gPINN: augments PINN loss with residual-gradient terms; hard boundary constraints; combined with RAR | 2–3x higher computational cost; extra gradient-loss weight hyperparameter to tune | gPINN adds PDE residual gradients to loss; higher accuracy with fewer points, ideal for steep gradients; combinable with RAR. |
| 5 | Self-Adaptive Physics-Informed Neural Networks using a Soft Attention Mechanism | arXiv:2009.04544 / 2020 | SA-PINN: per-point trainable self-adaptation weights (soft attention); gradient descent/ascent saddle-point training; NTK analysis | Extra hyperparameters (mask shape, weight LR, initialization); GP extension adds complexity | SA-PINN with per-point trainable attention weights; auto-focuses on stiff regions via saddle-point training and smooths NTK spectrum. |
| 6 | NSFnets (Navier-Stokes Flow nets): Physics-informed neural networks for the incompressible Navier-Stokes equations | arXiv:2003.06496 / 2020 | PINN with velocity-pressure (VP) and vorticity-velocity (VV) formulations; dynamic gradient-statistics-based loss weighting | Very expensive training (limits turbulence to subdomains); large pressure errors (15–19%); VV failed on turbulent DNS data | NSFnets for incompressible Navier-Stokes (VP/VV formulations); learns velocity, pressure, vorticity without pressure data. |
| 7 | A comprehensive study of non-adaptive and residual-based adaptive sampling for physics-informed neural networks | arXiv:2207.10289 / 2022 | Systematic comparison of 10 sampling strategies; proposes RAD (residual-based adaptive distribution) and RAR-D hybrid | Extra hyperparameters (k, c, periods); RAD costlier per iteration; little gain on smooth problems | Benchmarks 10 residual sampling schemes; proposes RAD and RAR-D adaptive sampling for higher accuracy with fewer points. |
| 8 | On the eigenvector bias of Fourier feature networks: From regression to solving multi-scale PDEs with physics-informed neural networks | arXiv:2012.10047 / 2020 | NTK theory/eigendecomposition; random Fourier feature embeddings; multi-scale (mFF) and spatio-temporal (ST-mFF) architectures | σ choice problem-dependent; NTK assumptions (infinite width, vanishing LR) only approximate | NTK analysis explains PINN spectral bias; Fourier feature networks (mFF/ST-mFF) resolve multi-scale and high-frequency PDEs. |
| 9 | PINNacle: A Comprehensive Benchmark of Physics-Informed Neural Networks for Solving PDEs | arXiv:2306.08827 / 2023 | 22-task benchmark dataset; ~10 SOTA methods on DeepXDE backend; multi-GPU training; standardized metrics (L2RE, Fourier error) | Most methods fail on high-dimensional/long-time/chaotic problems; results hyperparameter sensitive | PINNacle: 22-PDE benchmark with ~10 SOTA methods on DeepXDE; standardized L2/Fourier metrics reveal failures on complex cases. |
| 10 | DeepONet: Learning nonlinear operators for identifying differential equations based on the universal approximation theorem of operators | arXiv:1910.03193 / 2020 | Deep operator networks (branch/trunk); universal approximation theorem for operators; GRF/Chebyshev input spaces | Requires consistent sensor locations; theory bounds only approximation error; no results on required network size | DeepONet (branch + trunk) learns nonlinear operators from small data; universal approximation with high-order convergence. |
| 11 | Reliable extrapolation of deep neural operators informed by physics or sparse observations | arXiv:2212.06347 / 2022 | DeepONet + 2-Wasserstein distance for extrapolation complexity; fine-tuning (FT-Phys, FT-Obs) & multifidelity (MFGPR, MFNN) | Needs extra info (PDEs/observations); fine-tuning alone overfits/forgets; W2 scaling empirical | Quantifies DeepONet extrapolation via 2-Wasserstein distance; five fine-tuning/multifidelity methods ensure reliable OOD prediction. |
| 12 | Operator learning for predicting multiscale bubble growth dynamics | arXiv:2012.12816 / 2020 | DeepONet operator regression for Rayleigh–Plesset (macroscale) and DPD (microscale); transfer learning with sparse data | Extrapolation error rises for unseen correlation lengths; captures only mean, not fluctuations; needs ~5000 trajectories | DeepONet unifies macro (Rayleigh-Plesset) and micro (DPD) bubble dynamics; simplifies multiscale modeling without hand-shaking. |
| 13 | A physics-informed variational DeepONet for predicting the crack path in brittle materials | arXiv:2108.06905 / 2021 | V-DeepONet: variational energy-based loss + labeled IGA phase-field data; hybrid loss for fracture surrogates | Needs variational energy formulation; still needs high-fidelity IGA data; more complex pipeline than vanilla DeepONet | V-DeepONet combines variational energy loss with IGA data for brittle fracture; predicts damage/displacement for any crack config. |
| 14 | DeepM&Mnet: Inferring the electroconvection multiphysics fields based on operator approximation by neural networks | arXiv:2009.12935 / 2020 | DeepM&Mnet: multiple pre-trained DeepONets as plug-and-play constraints on FNN with sparse measurements | Requires pre-trained DeepONets from labeled data; inherits their bias; 500k iterations, careful loss weighting | DeepM&Mnet plugs multiple pre-trained DeepONets into FNN; fast multiphysics electroconvection inference from sparse measurements. |
| 15 | A comprehensive and fair comparison of two neural operators (with practical extensions) based on FAIR data | arXiv:2111.05512 / 2021 | 16-benchmark fair comparison: DeepONet vs FNO with extensions (POD-DeepONet, dFNO+/gFNO+); FAIR data principles | FNO needs equispaced grids/same domains/full-field data; FNO unstable with noise; large study hard to reproduce | Fair 16-task comparison: DeepONet vs FNO (+POD/dFNO+/gFNO+); FNO fails under noise/complex geometry, DeepONet robust. |
| 16 | Learning the solution operator of parametric partial differential equations with physics-informed DeepONets | arXiv:2103.10974 / 2021 | Physics-informed DeepONet: PDE regularization via automatic differentiation as soft penalties; Fourier feature embeddings | Requires known PDE form and careful loss weighting; standard MLP trunks fail without Fourier features | Physics-informed DeepONet adds PDE soft constraints; solves parametric PDEs without paired data, 1000x faster than solvers. |
| 17 | Multifidelity deep neural operators for efficient learning of partial differential equations with application to fast inverse design of nanoscale heat transport | arXiv:2204.06684 / 2022 | Multifidelity DeepONet: residual learning + input augmentation (low-fidelity as extra branch/trunk inputs); GA + topology optimization | Error bounded by low-fidelity + residual errors; only one augmentation approach helps; no accuracy guarantee for continuous designs | Multifidelity DeepONet via residual learning + input augmentation; 10x less high-fidelity data for phonon BTE inverse design. |
| 18 | MIONet: Learning multiple-input operators via tensor product | arXiv:2202.06137 / 2022 | Multiple-input operator learning; tensor product/low-rank theory; universal approximation on product Banach spaces | Large networks for many inputs; assumes compact subsets/continuity; longer training and more data than single-input operators | MIONet learns multi-input operators via tensor product; proves universal approximation on product Banach spaces. |
| 19 | Fourier-DeepONet: Fourier-enhanced deep operator networks for full waveform inversion with improved accuracy, generalizability, and robustness | arXiv:2305.17289 / 2023 | Fourier-DeepONet: U-FNO decoder (FNO + U-Net) replacing inner-product decoder; source parameters as trunk input | Requires extensive labeled FWI datasets; U-FNO adds complexity/cost; robustness beyond tested ranges not guaranteed | Fourier-DeepONet (U-FNO decoder) for FWI; generalizes to varying seismic source frequencies/locations, noise-robust. |
| 20 | Fourier-MIONet: Fourier-enhanced multiple-input neural operators for multiphase modeling of geological carbon sequestration | arXiv:2303.04778 / 2023-2024 | Fourier-MIONet: MIONet + U-FNO architecture for CO2-brine flow; time-continuity physics prior | Depends on high-fidelity simulation data; accuracy limited for highly nonlinear flows; OOD shown only for specific scenarios | Fourier-MIONet (MIONet+U-FNO) for CO2 sequestration; 90% fewer params, 3.5x faster than U-FNO, time-continuous predictions. |
| 21 | Scalable Uncertainty Quantification for Deep Operator Networks using Randomized Priors | arXiv:2203.03048 / 2022 | UQDeepONet: frequentist posterior UQ via ensembles of randomized-prior networks; vectorized JAX implementation | Ensemble training multiplies cost; quality depends on prior/ensemble design; mostly synthetic validation | UQDeepONet: randomized-prior ensembles with JAX for scalable posterior UQ; detects OOD/adversarial, handles scarce multiscale data. |
| 22 | D2NO: Efficient Handling of Heterogeneous Input Function Spaces with Distributed Deep Neural Operators | arXiv:2310.18888 / 2023 | Distributed/federated DeepONet: per-client branch nets (own sensors) + shared trunk; universal approximation theory | Requires careful client/task partitioning; shared trunk limits flexibility for dissimilar bases; only 4 examples validated | D2NO: distributed DeepONet with per-client branches + shared trunk for heterogeneous sensors; universal approximator, efficient. |
| 23 | Convergence Rate of DeepONets for Learning Operators Arising from Advection-Diffusion Equations | arXiv:2102.10621 / 2021 | Convergence-rate analysis; ReLU/rational-function branch networks; finite-difference "blessed representations" | Covers approximation error only; restricted to specific operator classes (advection-diffusion); smoothness assumptions | Convergence theory for DeepONets on advection-diffusion operators; rates depend on branch architecture and smoothness. |
| 24 | Physics-Informed Neural Networks with Hard Constraints for Inverse Design | arXiv:2102.04626 / 2021 | hPINN: hard constraints via penalty/augmented Lagrangian; exact BC enforcement via architecture modification | Penalty/augmented-Lagrangian tuning nontrivial; only small 2D problems shown; training cost may exceed adjoint solvers | hPINN enforces hard constraints via penalty/augmented Lagrangian for topology optimization; smoother designs without PDE solver. |
| 25 | Parallel Physics-Informed Neural Networks via Domain Decomposition | arXiv:2104.10013 / 2021 | cPINN/XPINN: domain decomposition in space/space-time; MPI+X hybrid parallelism (CPU/GPU) | cPINN limited to space decomposition/conservation laws; interface conditions & load balancing needed; communication overhead grows | cPINN/XPINN domain decomposition (space/space-time) with MPI+X parallelism; per-subdomain tuning for multiscale problems. |
| 26 | Communication-Efficient Learning of Deep Networks from Decentralized Data | AISTATS 2017 / arXiv:1602.05629 | Federated Learning; FedAvg (local SGD + server averaging); client sampling, local epochs, minibatching | High local epochs can plateau/divergence; privacy depends on update content; averaging non-convex models can be poor | FedAvg: local SGD + server averaging for decentralized deep learning; 10-100x fewer communication rounds vs sync SGD. |
| 27 | Privacy-preserving Federated Brain Tumour Segmentation | 2019 / BraTS 2018 | FedAvg + differential privacy (selective parameter sharing, sparse vector technique, Laplacian noise) | Clear privacy-performance trade-off; overhead when momentum aggregated; conservative privacy-cost; BraTS-only evaluation | Federated brain tumour segmentation on BraTS with DP (sparse vector, selective sharing); highlights privacy-performance trade-off. |
| 28 | Federated Learning for Internet of Things: A Comprehensive Survey | IEEE Commun. Surv. Tutor., Vol. 23, No. 3, pp. 1622–1658, 2021 (DOI: 10.1109/COMST.2021.3075439) | Survey of FL in IoT: taxonomies (horizontal/vertical/transfer), blockchain, homomorphic encryption, DP, edge computing | Survey only — no new algorithm/empirical validation; descriptive rather than rigorous comparison; may become dated | Survey of FL for IoT: services, taxonomies, and apps (healthcare, transport, UAV, smart city); future challenges. |
| 29 | Federated Learning with Differential Privacy: Algorithms and Performance Analysis | IEEE Trans. Inf. | 30 | Differentially Private Federated Learning: A Client Level Perspective | ICLR 2019 / arXiv:1712.07557 | Client-level DP: curator clips updates, adds Gaussian noise, moments accountant; random client sub-sampling | Significant degradation for small client pools (78% at 100); clipping bound computed non-privately; heavy grid search | Client-level DP FL via clipped updates + Gaussian noise + moments accountant; privacy at low cost with many clients. |
| 31 | Measuring the Effects of Non-Identical Data Distribution for Federated Visual Classification | NeurIPS FL Workshop 2019 / arXiv:1909.06335 | Dirichlet-synthesized non-IID client populations; FedAvgM with server-side Nesterov momentum | Still degrades with more non-IID data; extra β hyperparameter, narrow effective LR window; single architecture/CIFAR-10 | Dirichlet non-IID synthesis for FL vision; FedAvgM (server momentum) boosts CIFAR-10 30% to 77% under extreme skew. |
| 32 | Adaptive Federated Optimization | ICLR 2021 / arXiv:2003.00295 | FedOpt framework: adaptive server optimizers (FedAdagrad, FedAdam, FedYogi); per-coordinate adaptivity | Extra adaptivity parameter τ to tune; heterogeneity still hurts; no control-variate mechanism (vs SCAFFOLD) | FedOpt: federated Adagrad/Adam/Yogi with server adaptivity; improves tuning and convergence on heterogeneous non-convex tasks. |
| 33 | Advances and Open Problems in Federated Learning | Found. Trends Mach. | 34 | Federated Learning with Non-IID Data | arXiv:1806.00582 / 2018 | FedAvg + weight divergence analysis; EMD bound; global class-balanced data-sharing strategy | Data sharing partially centralizes data (privacy trade-off); identical initialization assumption; restrictive Lipschitz bounds | Quantifies non-IID via EMD weight divergence (up to 55% drop); 5% shared data recovers 30% accuracy on CIFAR-10. |
| 35 | Federated Optimization in Heterogeneous Networks (FedProx) | MLSys 2020 / arXiv:1812.06127 | FedProx: proximal term in local objective; γ-inexact local solutions; B-local dissimilarity assumption | Sufficient-not-necessary conditions; no better than distributed SGD rates; µ tuning; can slow convergence on IID data | FedProx adds proximal term for systems + statistical heterogeneity; allows variable local work, +22% over FedAvg when heterogeneous. |
| 36 | On the Convergence of FedAvg on Non-IID Data | ICLR 2020 / arXiv:1907.02189 | O(1/T) convergence for FedAvg on non-IID; Γ heterogeneity quantification; LR-decay necessity proof | Restricted to strongly convex/smooth problems; FedAvg lacks linear speedup in non-IID settings | FedAvg O(1/T) convergence on non-IID (strongly convex); shows LR decay needed and heterogeneity slows convergence. |
| 37 | POT: Python Optimal Transport | JMLR 2021 | Open-source Python toolbox: Sinkhorn, Wasserstein barycenters, Gromov-Wasserstein, partial OT, domain adaptation | GeomLoss/KeOps better for very large-scale Sinkhorn; PyTorch/TF native backends incomplete at publication | POT: Python Optimal Transport toolbox (Sinkhorn, barycenters, Gromov-Wasserstein); MIT-licensed ML toolkit. |
